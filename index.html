See the platform in action

A real flow: a workshop on the board becomes initiatives, epics and stories in Planning, followed on Kanban boards and reports.

Benefits

Clarity, collaboration, organisation and follow-up

Clarity

Discussions become visible boards: frames, post-its, flows and decisions recorded with comments and votes.

Collaboration

Everyone on the same board in real time – live cursors, comments, dot voting and a shared timer for workshops.

Organisation

Each item has an owner, a due date, a priority and a status – on a roadmap, schedule, Scrum or Kanban.

Follow-up

Sprint reports, burndown, OKRs with progress and e-mail alerts when a deadline is near or late.

The base of the platform

Visual collaboration and planning, connected

Included in every plan. Available in English, Portuguese (Brazil and Portugal) and Spanish – on web, tablet and phone.

Visual collaboration

Build ideas, processes and decisions together.

  • Infinite canvas with frames, shapes, connectors, images and live cursors
  • 50+ templates: Design & UX, Design Thinking, processes, product, architecture and executive meetings
  • AI assistant that builds the board, suggests dynamics and analyses the results
  • A process step becomes an item in a planning with one click

Planning and execution

Manage projects, tasks, priorities, owners and deadlines.

  • Jira-style roadmap, Waterfall schedule, Scrum and Kanban
  • Several boards per planning: basic, upstream, downstream and sprint
  • OKR → initiative → epic → story with inherited progress
  • Reports, budget vs. actual and e-mail alerts per card
  • Import and sync with Jira, Azure DevOps and Kanbanize
Add-on modules

Modules with their own goals

Separate licences, for a whole company or per person – and both connect to Planning.

Service management – ITSM

Organise service requests and support routines, with visibility of demands, owners and service levels.

  • Requester portal and agent queue, with Kanban boards
  • Incidents, requests, problems and changes (with calendar)
  • SLAs with business hours and pause, catalogue, knowledge base and CMDB
  • Based on ITIL 4 practices; approved changes become milestones in the roadmap

Business Game

Apply business simulations in courses and training, so participants practise decisions and analyse their results.

  • Rounds with decisions on price, production, marketing, R&D, people, investment and financing
  • Income statement, balance sheet, cash flow and team ranking per round
  • Professor panel: open and close rounds, deadlines, events and exports
  • Ready-made scenarios, any currency and the institution's brand

Occupational health

Manage health, safety and the environment (SHE) in one place, separate from the IT service desk.

  • Records with workflow and deadlines: accidents and CAT, near misses, PPE, exams and absences
  • SHE: environmental incidents, inspections and non-conformities
  • Panel with days without accidents, frequency and severity rates
  • Templates: occupational health programme, safety committee, emergency plan, ISO 14001 and HSE goals

Examples of use

For different areas and team sizes

Professionals and small teams

Organise tasks, projects and recurring activities on a Kanban board, with due dates and alerts.

Project and process managers

Plan deliveries on a roadmap or schedule, map flows on the board and follow who is responsible for what.

Software development teams

Plan sprints and backlogs, follow execution in reports and keep in sync with Jira, Azure DevOps or Kanbanize.

Marketing, education, health and other areas

Adapt boards and planning to your routine – campaign calendars, course launches or administrative and operational projects in health.

Companies and service teams

Run requests, incidents and changes in the ITSM module, within its available scope.

Universities and corporate training

Use the Business Game in learning activities and simulations, with rounds and results for each team.

Mentors and consultants

Run sessions, organise priorities with MoSCoW and RICE, and follow action plans with the people you guide.
