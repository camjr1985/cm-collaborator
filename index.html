Features

From the workshop to the delivery – everything connected

Available in English, Portuguese (Brazil and Portugal) and Spanish – on web, tablet and phone.

AI whiteboards

Infinite canvas, live cursors, comments and voting – plus an AI assistant that builds the board, suggests dynamics and analyses the results.

50+ professional templates

Design & UX, Design Thinking, processes, product management, software and security architecture, data and executive meetings.

Jira-style planning

Roadmap, Waterfall schedule, Scrum and Kanban with upstream and downstream – several boards per planning, drag and drop.

OKRs and reports

OKR → initiative → epic → story with inherited progress; sprint reports, burndown and budget vs. actual.

Alerts and integrations

E-mail alerts per card and per planning; two-way sync with Jira, Azure DevOps and Kanbanize.

Add-on modules

ITSM (ITIL 4) and Business Game, licensed per company or per user and connectable to Planning.

How it works

From the idea to the delivery in 3 steps

1 · Create your workspace

Invite your team, choose your area (health, retail, consulting, education, service management) and start from a template.

2 · Plan and collaborate

Run workshops on the boards and turn ideas into initiatives, epics and stories in Planning – everything stays connected.

3 · Track and decide

Follow boards, OKRs and reports, get alerts when something is late and decide with data.
